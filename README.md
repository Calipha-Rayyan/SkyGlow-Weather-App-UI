## 📖 Project Overview

**SkyGlow Weather App** is a contemporary mobile weather application interface designed during **Week 2 of the UI/UX Design Internship**. 

Weather applications often suffer from either information overload or excessive visual clutter. SkyGlow addresses this challenge by blending **atmospheric visual hierarchy**, **clean micro-data cards**, and **dynamic weather-state cues** into a fluid, user-friendly mobile experience.

### 🎯 Key Design Goals
- **Instant Glanceability:** Deliver crucial weather parameters (temperature, condition, high/low, precipitation) in under 2 seconds.
- **Visual Harmony:** Balance vibrant atmospheric gradients with clean minimalist typography and accessible contrast.
- **Predictive & Contextual Data:** Seamless transitions between real-time conditions, hourly trends, and comprehensive 7-day outlooks.
- **Thoughtful Edge States:** Provide intuitive empty states and seamless search navigation for smooth user flows.

## ✨ Features & UX Highlights

- 🌡️ **Dynamic Temperature Card:** Clear focal point highlighting current temperature with contextual "feels like" metrics.
- ⏱️ **Interactive Hourly Scroller:** Horizontal carousel allowing users to scrub through 24-hour forecasts smoothly.
- 📅 **7-Day Trend Visualization:** Min/Max temperature indicators providing immediate long-term weather planning context.
- 🧭 **Multi-Location Search:** Fast search UX with recent city chips and instant suggestions.
- 📊 **Metric Detail Grid:** Card-based modular layout for Air Quality Index (AQI), UV index, Humidity, Sunrise/Sunset, and Pressure.
- 💡 **Helpful Zero States:** Delightful illustration and empathetic copy ensuring users never feel stuck during zero-result searches.

## 🎨 Design System & Guidelines

### 🔤 Typography
- **Primary Typeface:** Clean, modern Sans-Serif optimized for digital screens and mobile legibility.
- **Hierarchy:**
  - *Display / Hero (Temperatures):* Bold, heavy weight for rapid focal recognition.
  - *Headings & City Names:* Semi-bold, medium tracking for clear navigational context.
  - *Data & Metrics / Body:* Regular / Medium with high-contrast neutral tones for readability.

### 🌈 Color Palette & Mood
- **Atmospheric Gradients:** Reflect daylight, twilight, overcast, and night skies dynamically.
- **Primary Brand Accents:** Luminous sky blues, deep navy bases, and soft ambient glows.
- **Semantic Indicators:** Contextual hues for rain chance, UV levels, and air quality categories.
- **Contrast & Accessibility:** Adheres to WCAG AA contrast standards for legible text across varied ambient lighting.

### 📐 Layout & Components
- **8pt Grid System:** Strict spacing consistency across margins, gutters, and card paddings.
- **Auto-Layout Components:** Responsive cards, modular list items, and scalable iconography built directly in Figma.
